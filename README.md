# Netflix-churn-analysis
I developed a Netflix Customer Churn Analysis dashboard using Power BI to understand customer retention patterns and identify customer segments associated with higher churn.
I used Power Query for data preparation and DAX to calculate KPIs such as total customers, churned customers, churn rate, and revenue-related metrics. I created interactive dashboards to analyze churn by subscription type, region, gender, age group, number of profiles, and payment method.

The analysis showed an overall churn rate of 50.3% in the dataset. I also observed differences in churn counts across subscription plans and regions, along with variations in churn rates across age groups and payment methods.

The objective of the project was to convert raw customer data into meaningful business insights and recommend areas for further investigation to improve customer retention.
